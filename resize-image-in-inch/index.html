How to use this tool

An image resizer changes the width and height of a photo while keeping the result suitable for web, forms, documents and applications. FormTools lets you work in pixels, centimetres, millimetres or inches and choose a DPI value for physical-size workflows.

Resize by PX, CM, MM or Inch

Use PX when a website asks for exact pixel dimensions. Use CM, MM or Inch when a printed or application form specifies physical dimensions. DPI connects physical dimensions with pixels.

DPI options

Choose 72, 96, 150, 200, 300, 600 or a custom DPI. For example, a 35 × 45 mm image at 300 DPI is approximately 413 × 531 pixels.

More controls

  • Maintain aspect ratio or set width and height independently.
  • Crop to common ratios before resizing.
  • Rotate 90°, 180° or 270° and flip horizontally or vertically.
  • Export as JPG, PNG or WebP and adjust quality.
  • Compress toward 20 KB, 50 KB, 100 KB or a custom target.
  • Process multiple images and download them together as a ZIP when supported by the browser.

Privacy

Core image processing is performed in the browser. Your selected images are not sent to a FormTools image-processing server by this tool.

Frequently asked questions

Can I resize an image in CM or MM?

Yes. Select CM or MM, enter the physical width and height, and choose the DPI used for the pixel conversion.

What DPI should I use?

Use the DPI required by your application or printer. 300 DPI is a common print-oriented setting, but the required value depends on the destination.

Can I compress an image to 20 KB?

Yes. Select a 20 KB target or open the dedicated 20 KB compression page. The browser adjusts quality and, when necessary, dimensions to get at or below the target.

Does the image leave my device?

The core resize and compression work is performed locally in your browser.

Can I process many images?

Yes. Multiple image selection is supported, with a ZIP download when the browser can load the ZIP helper.

Contact FormTools

If you find a broken tool, incorrect output, missing feature or another issue, contact the FormTools team so it can be reviewed.

What to include

  • The tool URL you were using.
  • Your browser and device type.
  • The input format and approximate file size, without sending private documents unless necessary.
  • The error message or a screenshot when useful.

Tool suggestions and feedback

You can also contact us with requests for new image, PDF, cyber-café or application-file utilities.

Privacy

Please do not include passwords, identity numbers, financial information or other sensitive information in a support message.

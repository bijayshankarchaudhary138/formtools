## Search-volume note
Public web search results do not expose reliable monthly search-volume numbers. Do not invent volumes. Exact monthly volume should be pulled from Google Keyword Planner, Search Console after indexing, or a dedicated keyword database (Ahrefs/Semrush/Keyword Planner). The priority labels above are intent/competition strategy, not fabricated volume figures.

## PI7 strategy verified
PI7 currently exposes direct target-size pages, range pages, CM/MM/PX resizing, DPI, maintain-aspect-ratio, crop/rotate/zoom, bulk image handling, passport presets, and exact-size PDF pages. It also has exam-specific pages such as GATE photo/signature. FormTools V20 adds corresponding SEO landing pages while keeping requirements honest where they vary by notification.
